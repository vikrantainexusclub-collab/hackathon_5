Problem Statements

01
Intelligent Academic Planning & Student Success Platform
Category: Software + AI/ML + Cloud Computing + Data Analytics
Theme: AI-Driven Smart Education and Student Success Management
An Intelligent Academic Planning & Student Success Platform is a smart web-based system that helps students effectively plan their academic journey. It provides personalized course recommendations, semester-wise planning, and progress tracking based on students' academic performance. The platform uses data analytics to identify at-risk students and suggests timely interventions to improve learning outcomes. It also enables faculty to monitor student performance through interactive dashboards and reports. Overall, the system enhances academic decision-making, improves student success, and supports efficient educational management.
02
Smart Healthcare Assistance & Patient Monitoring System
Category: Software + Hardware (AI, IoT, Cloud Computing, Healthcare Technology)
Theme: AI-Enabled Smart Healthcare and Remote Patient Monitoring
The Smart Healthcare Assistance & Patient Monitoring System continuously monitors patients' vital signs using IoT-enabled sensors and wearable devices. It analyzes health data in real time using AI algorithms to detect abnormalities and predict potential medical risks. The system sends instant alerts to doctors, caregivers, or family members during emergencies. Cloud technology securely stores patient records and enables remote access for healthcare professionals. This solution improves patient care, supports telemedicine, and enhances healthcare efficiency and accessibility.
03
Sustainable Agriculture Advisory Platform
Category: Software + AI + IoT + Cloud Computing + Data Analytics
Theme: AI-Driven Precision Agriculture and Sustainable Farming
The Sustainable Agriculture Advisory Platform is an intelligent system that helps farmers make informed decisions to improve crop productivity and sustainability. It uses AI, IoT sensors, weather data, and soil analysis to provide personalized recommendations for irrigation, fertilization, and pest management. The platform monitors field conditions in real time and generates timely alerts to prevent crop loss. Cloud technology enables secure data storage and remote access to farming insights. Overall, the system promotes resource-efficient farming, reduces environmental impact, and supports sustainable agricultural practices.
04
Intelligent Transportation & Traffic Optimization System Category
Category: Software + AI + IoT + Cloud Computing + Data Analytics
Theme: AI-Driven Intelligent Transportation and Smart Traffic Management
The Intelligent Transportation & Traffic Optimization System is a smart solution designed to improve traffic flow and enhance road safety. It uses AI, IoT sensors, GPS data, and real-time traffic analytics to monitor road conditions and optimize traffic signal timings. The system detects congestion, predicts traffic patterns, and suggests alternative routes to commuters. Cloud technology enables centralized data management and real-time access for traffic authorities. Overall, the platform reduces travel time, minimizes fuel consumption, and supports the development of smart and sustainable cities.
05
Digital Tourism & Cultural Heritage Experience
Category: Software + AI + AR/VR + Cloud Computing + Mobile Technology
Theme: AI-Enabled Digital Tourism and Cultural Heritage Preservation
The Digital Tourism & Cultural Heritage Experience platform enhances travel by providing interactive and immersive experiences for tourists. It uses AI, Augmented Reality (AR), and Virtual Reality (VR) to showcase historical sites, museums, and cultural landmarks through virtual tours and smart guides. The system offers personalized travel recommendations, multilingual support, and location-based information. Cloud technology enables secure storage and easy access to tourism content from anywhere. Overall, the platform promotes cultural heritage preservation, improves tourist engagement, and supports the growth of smart tourism.
06
Smart Waste Management & Environmental Monitoring Category
Category: Software + Hardware (AI, IoT, Cloud Computing, Environmental Monitoring)
Theme:AI-Driven Smart Waste Management and Environmental Sustainability
The Smart Waste Management & Environmental Monitoring System is designed to improve waste collection efficiency and monitor environmental conditions in real time. It uses IoT-enabled smart bins and environmental sensors to track waste levels, air quality, temperature, and humidity. AI analyzes the collected data to optimize waste collection routes and identify potential environmental risks. Cloud technology provides centralized data storage, monitoring dashboards, and instant alerts for authorities. Overall, the system promotes cleaner cities, efficient resource management, and environmental sustainability.
07
Disaster Preparedness & Emergency Response Platform Category
Category: Software + AI + IoT + GIS + Cloud Computing
Theme: AI-Enabled Disaster Management and Emergency Response
The Disaster Preparedness & Emergency Response Platform is a smart system designed to improve disaster prediction, preparedness, and response. It uses AI, IoT sensors, GIS mapping, and real-time weather data to detect potential disasters and assess risk levels. The platform sends instant alerts, evacuation guidance, and emergency notifications to affected communities and authorities. Cloud technology enables secure data sharing and coordination among emergency response teams. Overall, the system enhances public safety, reduces disaster impact, and supports efficient disaster management.
08
Citizen Engagement & Public Grievance Management Category
Category: Software + AI + Cloud Computing + Data Analytics + Mobile Technology
Theme: AI-Driven Digital Governance and Citizen-Centric Public Services
The Citizen Engagement & Public Grievance Management System is a digital platform that enables citizens to submit complaints, suggestions, and service requests efficiently. It uses AI to categorize grievances, prioritize urgent cases, and provide automated responses and status updates. The platform offers real-time complaint tracking, analytics dashboards, and transparent communication between citizens and government authorities. Cloud technology ensures secure data storage and seamless accessibility across devices. Overall, the system improves public service delivery, enhances transparency, and strengthens citizen participation in governance.
09
Future Workforce & Career Guidance Ecosystem Category
Category: Software + AI + Cloud Computing + Data Analytics + Machine Learning
Theme: AI-Driven Career Guidance and Future Workforce Development
The Future Workforce & Career Guidance Ecosystem is an intelligent platform that helps students and job seekers make informed career decisions based on their skills, interests, and industry trends. It uses AI and machine learning to recommend career paths, skill development programs, internships, and job opportunities. The platform analyzes market demand and identifies skill gaps to provide personalized learning and career guidance. Cloud technology enables secure data management and access from anywhere. Overall, the system enhances employability, supports lifelong learning, and prepares individuals for the evolving workforce.
10
Cyber Security & Digital Trust Framework Category
Category: Software + AI + Cyber Security + Cloud Computing + Blockchain
Theme: AI-Driven Cyber Security and Digital Trust Management
The Cyber Security & Digital Trust Framework is an intelligent platform designed to protect digital systems, networks, and sensitive data from cyber threats. It uses AI to detect suspicious activities, identify security vulnerabilities, and respond to cyber attacks in real time. Blockchain technology enhances data integrity, secure authentication, and digital trust across connected systems. Cloud computing enables centralized security monitoring, threat analysis, and incident management. Overall, the framework strengthens cyber resilience, safeguards digital assets, and promotes secure and trustworthy digital services.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Team size: Up to 4 members; at least 1 female member is compulsory..
  • Eligibility: Open to students, developers, and innovators across India.
  • Originality: All code and assets must be original or properly licensed. Strictly no plagiarism.
  • Rounds: Multiple coding rounds, mentor checkpoints, and evaluations.
  • Judging criteria: Innovation, impact, feasibility, user experience, and presentation.
  • Code of conduct: Respectful, inclusive collaboration is mandatory.
