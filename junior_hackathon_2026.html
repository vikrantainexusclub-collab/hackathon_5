INNOVIK JUNIOR HACKATHON 2026

INNOVIK Junior Hackathon 2026 is a one-day innovation competition specially designed for school students of Classes X, XI, and XII. The event provides young innovators with a platform to transform creative ideas into practical solutions for real-world challenges. Participants will work in teams to develop innovative prototypes, models, mobile applications, websites, AI-based solutions, or technology-driven concepts under the guidance of expert mentors. The hackathon aims to inspire creativity, critical thinking, teamwork, entrepreneurship, and future-ready skills among school students. The Junior Hackathon will be organized alongside INNOVIK 6.0 – International Hackathon 2026, allowing students to experience the atmosphere of a prestigious international innovation event.

This premier hackathon focuses on themes: Innovating Today for a Smarter Tomorrow

Problem Statements

01
Develop innovative ideas to improve classroom learning using technology.
Category: Education Technology (EdTech)
Theme: Smart Classroom of the Future
The Smart Classroom of the Future focuses on transforming traditional learning environments into intelligent, interactive, and technology-driven spaces. By integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI), the Internet of Things (IoT), Augmented/Virtual Reality (AR/VR), cloud computing, and data analytics, smart classrooms provide personalized learning experiences, real-time student performance monitoring, and enhanced collaboration between teachers and students. These classrooms support digital content, virtual laboratories, automated attendance, adaptive assessments, and remote learning, making education more engaging, accessible, and effective. The goal is to create a future-ready education system that develops critical thinking, creativity, problem-solving, and digital skills among learners.
02
AI for Education
Category:Education Technology (EdTech)
Theme: AI for Education
AI for Education focuses on leveraging Artificial Intelligence to enhance teaching, learning, and educational administration. AI-powered systems can deliver personalized learning experiences, automate assessments, provide intelligent tutoring, analyze student performance, and support educators with data-driven insights. By integrating technologies such as machine learning, natural language processing, and predictive analytics, AI helps improve learning outcomes, increase student engagement, and streamline academic processes. The objective is to build an inclusive, efficient, and future-ready education ecosystem that empowers both learners and educators.
03
Plastic-Free School
Category:Environment & Sustainability
Theme: Plastic-Free School
The Plastic-Free School theme promotes a clean, healthy, and eco-friendly learning environment by reducing or eliminating the use of single-use plastics on campus. It encourages sustainable practices such as using reusable water bottles, cloth bags, steel lunch boxes, paper-based alternatives, and effective waste segregation and recycling. Through awareness campaigns, student-led initiatives, and green policies, schools can foster environmental responsibility and inspire lasting behavioral change. The goal is to create a sustainable campus while educating students about the importance of protecting the environment for future generations.
04
Smart Water Conservation
Category:Environment & Sustainability
Theme: Smart Water Conservation
Smart Water Conservation focuses on using modern technologies to monitor, manage, and optimize water usage efficiently. By integrating IoT sensors, smart water meters, AI-based analytics, rainwater harvesting systems, and automated leak detection, organizations and communities can reduce water wastage and ensure sustainable water management. The theme promotes responsible water consumption, real-time monitoring, and data-driven decision-making to protect this vital resource. Its objective is to create a sustainable future by conserving water while meeting the growing needs of society.
05
Green & Clean School Campus
Category:Environment & Sustainability
Theme: Green & Clean School Campus
The Green & Clean School Campus theme promotes the development of environmentally friendly, clean, and sustainable educational institutions. It encourages initiatives such as tree plantation, waste segregation, recycling, energy conservation, water management, cleanliness drives, and the use of renewable energy. By involving students, teachers, and the community in eco-friendly practices, schools can create a healthier learning environment while fostering environmental awareness and social responsibility. The goal is to build sustainable campuses that inspire students to become responsible citizens and protect the environment for future generations.
06
Student Health & Fitness
Category:Health & Wellness
Theme:Student Health & Fitness
The Student Health & Fitness theme focuses on promoting the physical, mental, and emotional well-being of students through healthy lifestyle practices and technology-enabled wellness solutions. It encourages regular physical activity, balanced nutrition, mental health awareness, fitness tracking, preventive healthcare, and access to health education. Smart technologies such as fitness apps, wearable devices, AI-based health monitoring, and digital wellness platforms can help students develop healthy habits and improve overall well-being. The objective is to create a healthier, more active, and productive learning environment that supports students' academic success and personal growth.
07
Smart Attendance System
Category:Education Technology (EdTech)
Theme: Smart Attendance System
The Smart Attendance System is a technology-driven solution designed to automate and simplify student attendance management in educational institutions. Using technologies such as QR codes, RFID, facial recognition, biometric authentication, AI, and IoT, the system records attendance quickly, accurately, and securely. It eliminates manual registers, minimizes proxy attendance, and provides real-time attendance reports, notifications, and analytics for teachers, students, and parents. The objective is to improve efficiency, enhance transparency, reduce administrative workload, and support the development of smart, digitally enabled classrooms.
08
Cyber Safety for Students
Category:Cyber Security & Digital Safety
Theme:Cyber Safety for Students
The Cyber Safety for Students theme focuses on protecting students from online threats while promoting responsible and secure use of digital technologies. It encourages awareness of cyber risks such as phishing, cyberbullying, identity theft, online scams, malware, and privacy breaches. Through cybersecurity education, safe internet practices, strong password management, digital citizenship, and the responsible use of social media, students can develop the skills needed to navigate the online world safely. The objective is to create a secure digital learning environment where students can confidently use technology while protecting their personal information and online well-being.
09
Women Safety
Category:Safety & Security
Theme: Women Safety
The Women Safety theme focuses on developing innovative solutions that enhance the safety, security, and well-being of women in public and private spaces. It encourages the use of technologies such as mobile applications, GPS tracking, artificial intelligence, IoT devices, wearable safety gadgets, emergency alert systems, and smart surveillance to provide real-time assistance and rapid emergency response. The objective is to empower women with accessible safety tools, promote awareness, strengthen community support, and create safer environments through technology-driven solutions.
10
Smart Traffic & Road Safety
Category:Smart Cities & Transportation
Theme: Smart Traffic & Road Safety
The Smart Traffic & Road Safety theme focuses on improving traffic management and enhancing road safety through intelligent technologies. It encourages the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI), the Internet of Things (IoT), smart traffic signals, GPS, computer vision, and real-time data analytics to monitor traffic flow, detect violations, reduce congestion, and respond quickly to accidents. The theme also promotes safer driving practices, pedestrian protection, and efficient emergency response systems. Its objective is to create safer, smarter, and more sustainable transportation systems that reduce road accidents, improve mobility, and enhance the overall quality of urban life.
11
Digital India for Rural Communities
Category:Digital Inclusion & Rural Development
Theme: Digital India for Rural Communities
The Digital India for Rural Communities theme focuses on leveraging digital technologies to improve the quality of life and promote inclusive development in rural areas. It encourages innovative solutions in e-governance, digital education, telemedicine, agriculture, financial inclusion, skill development, and access to government services. By using technologies such as mobile applications, cloud computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI), the Internet of Things (IoT), and digital payment systems, rural communities can gain better access to essential services and economic opportunities. The objective is to bridge the digital divide, empower rural citizens, and support sustainable, technology-driven development across villages and underserved regions.
12
Waste Management using AI
Category:Environment & Sustainability
Theme: Waste Management using AI
The Waste Management using AI theme focuses on applying Artificial Intelligence (AI) to improve waste collection, segregation, recycling, and disposal processes. AI-powered technologies such as computer vision, machine learning, smart sensors, and IoT can automatically identify and classify waste, optimize collection routes, predict waste generation, and enhance recycling efficiency. These intelligent solutions help reduce landfill waste, lower operational costs, improve resource utilization, and promote a cleaner environment. The objective is to build sustainable and smart waste management systems that support environmental conservation and the development of cleaner cities and communities.
13
Future School using Robotics & IoT
Category:Education Technology (EdTech)
Theme: Future School using Robotics & IoT
The Future School using Robotics & IoT theme explores the integration of robotics and the Internet of Things (IoT) to create intelligent, interactive, and technology-enabled learning environments. It encourages the use of smart classrooms, educational robots, connected devices, automated attendance systems, intelligent laboratories, and real-time monitoring to enhance teaching and learning experiences. These technologies foster hands-on learning, creativity, problem-solving, collaboration, and STEM education while improving campus safety and operational efficiency. The objective is to build future-ready schools that prepare students with the digital and technical skills needed for the next generation of innovation and Industry 4.0.
14
Disaster Management & Emergency Response
Category:Public Safety & Disaster Management
Theme: Disaster Management & Emergency Response
The Disaster Management & Emergency Response theme focuses on developing innovative technologies that improve disaster preparedness, early warning, emergency communication, rescue operations, and post-disaster recovery. It encourages the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI), the Internet of Things (IoT), Geographic Information Systems (GIS), drones, satellite data, mobile applications, and real-time analytics to detect risks, monitor affected areas, coordinate emergency services, and provide timely assistance. The objective is to minimize the impact of natural and human-made disasters, protect lives and infrastructure, and build resilient communities through smart, technology-driven emergency response systems.
15
Open Innovation
Category: Innovation & Emerging Technologies
Theme: Open Innovation
The Open Innovation theme encourages participants to identify real-world problems and develop creative, technology-driven solutions without being limited to a specific domain. It welcomes innovative ideas across areas such as education, healthcare, agriculture, smart cities, environment, cybersecurity, finance, accessibility, sustainability, and social impact. Participants are free to leverage emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), the Internet of Things (IoT), Blockchain, Cloud Computing, Robotics, Augmented/Virtual Reality (AR/VR), and Data Analytics to design impactful solutions. The objective is to foster creativity, interdisciplinary collaboration, and innovation that addresses societal, industrial, or global challenges while creating meaningful value for communities and businesses.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Team size: Up to 4 members;
  • Eligibility:
    • Open only for School Students.
    • Classes X, XI & XII.
    • CBSE, ICSE and State Board Schools.
    • Team Size: 2–4 Students.
    • One School Mentor/Teacher (Optional).
  • Originality: All code and assets must be original or properly licensed. Strictly no plagiarism.
  • Rounds: Multiple coding rounds, mentor checkpoints, and evaluations.
  • Judging criteria: Innovation, impact, feasibility, user experience, and presentation.
  • Code of conduct: Respectful, inclusive collaboration is mandatory.
